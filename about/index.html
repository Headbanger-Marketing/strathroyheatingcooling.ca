Rooted in Strathroy. Ready for Every Ontario Season.

Our Story

A Middlesex County HVAC Company Built on Local Trust

We live where you live, and we understand what homes in Strathroy actually go through.

Strathroy sits in the heart of Middlesex County, about 40 kilometres west of London, and the homes here face real Ontario weather. Century homes near the Sydenham River, post-war bungalows, and newer builds out toward Komoka and Mount Brydges each come with their own heating and cooling quirks. Our crew has worked in all of them, and we treat every service call with the same careful attention we'd give a neighbour's house.

We're a family-run business, not a call-centre chain. When the phone rings at (548) 708-7117, you're speaking with people who know the difference between a February deep-freeze and a sticky August stretch, and who understand that a furnace failure in January is more than an inconvenience, it's a genuine safety concern for your whole household.

From our home base on Metcalfe Street East, we serve Strathroy, London, St. Thomas, Dorchester, Komoka, Aylmer, and Lucan, communities we know by name, not just by postal code. That local footing matters: it means faster drive times, honest pricing, and a contractor who will still be here next season when you need us again.

Our Commitment

Every job we take on, from a high-efficiency furnace install in an older Strathroy home to a ductless mini-split in a finished basement near London, is completed to code, with equipment and workmanship we stand behind.
