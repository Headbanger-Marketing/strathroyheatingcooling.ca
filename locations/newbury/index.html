HVAC Service in Newbury, Ontario

Newbury is a small rural village in northwest Middlesex County, set between Strathroy and Parkhill, and known as one of the smallest incorporated villages in Ontario. The surrounding farms and country homes rely heavily on propane and oil heat through long winters, with natural gas unavailable across most of the township. Strathroy Heating & Cooling services propane furnaces, oil units, and cold-climate heat pumps for Newbury area properties, with crews that already work the Strathroy to Parkhill corridor daily.

Newbury HVAC Questions

Do you service propane furnaces on farms near Newbury?

Yes. Most Newbury area properties heat with propane or oil off the gas main, and our technicians service those systems across the surrounding Middlesex farmland.

How far is Newbury from your Strathroy crew?

Newbury sits about 25 km north of Strathroy, right on our regular service route toward Parkhill, so we treat it as local and respond quickly.

Can you replace an oil furnace with a heat pump in Newbury?

Yes. Cold-climate heat pumps are a strong fit for Newbury homes looking to move off oil, and we size them to carry the full heating load through Middlesex winters.
